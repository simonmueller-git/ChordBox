The panel: left = chords, middle = bass

Left: MAIN knob (chords)

turnchord page (rolls through every bank)
clickthe chord MENU on the screen: turn = move, click = open / done · past 16: PERFORM. The pads keep playing.
holdcloses the MENU · when closed: MODES (turn + click picks)
pad + turnthat chord's inversion
pad + clickREPEAT that chord on the clock until you let go

A pad always plays its chord and brings the chord grid back: from the bass page, the MENU, MODES, SETUP. (Turning a MENU value: the pads play, the value stays open to hear it.) The first touch of MAIN or SHIFT also just brings the chords back.

Middle: only the bass

UP DOWNthe line before / after, heard straight away (with the Move running: from the next bar) · hold = fast
GENREtap: next genre, a line from it plays · hold: previous. 16 genres → ANY → FAVS
ON/OFFtap: bass on / off · hold: STAR the line (→ FAVS)
knob turnthe setting on the OLED's bottom line
knob clickthe next setting: OCTAVE −4…+2 · DENSITY · SWING · LENGTH · GATE · ACCENT · SLIDE · DUCK · FOLLOW · TEMPO
knob held = SHIFTPG UP PG DN 16 lines · RANDOM a surprise · NOTES (below) · held + turn = genre

Browsing (UP DOWN GENRE PG RANDOM) shows the bass page on the left: the 16 lines around you, each as its feel, its strongest trait, an energy meter and its bar of notes. ANY: DOWN picks a random genre; UP replays what you heard.

NOTES: find the key by ear

  • Middle knob held + NOTES: the 16 pads play single notes on the bass channel, C upwards (pad 1 = C1). The bass line rests meanwhile.
  • Play along with the Move. After a note: FITS (top button) or CLASH (second). Green = fits, red cross = clashes.
  • The screens show the key they point to, e.g. F minor 82%. Three or four answers are usually enough.
  • USE KEY (or middle click): use that key for chords and bass · middle turn = the pads' octave · ON/OFF = bass.
  • Knob held + LINES: the pads play chords again; the bass line comes back.

Reading the screens

  • Main screen: the chords (or the NOTES grid). Top: mode · key · Move clock · tempo. Bottom: MAIN (turn · click) left, the middle knob right. While browsing bass: the bass page, until a pad or MAIN.
  • OLED: genre (or ANY) · ON / MUTE / OFF · what the line is: its feel (ROLLING OFFBEAT ACID DOTTED SKIPPY GALLOP BOUNCY FUNKY DRIVING STEADY PUNCHY DEEP HELD SPARSE), traits (MELODIC ROOT OCTAVES SLIDES CHROMA LONG GHOSTS ACCENTS), energy 1–5 · its steps this bar · bottom: the knob's setting. NEXT = queued for the next bar.
  • In NOTES: the last note, the key guess, the 12 notes marked.

BASS mode (MODES → 2): the pads are the lines

The bass page, and here the pads pick its lines (tap = play from the next bar; hold + MAIN click = STAR). MAIN turn = page, click = back to CHORD. In a cell: taller bar = higher note · red = accent · line along the top = held · hollow = DENSITY skips it · dots = bar of a 2/4-bar line. Its MENU (MAIN hold) has EDIT, the step editor.

HUNT: the chords of a sample

  • MODES → 11 NEW SONG, then 3 HUNT. Middle knob on TUNE: hold a note, turn until the beating stops (old samples sit 20–50 cents off).
  • Tap the orange pad, answer FITS / CLASH (no press = unsure). Root + fifth probes find the 7 notes in 3–4 answers.
  • Per bar: find the lowest note that sits → YES → three chords containing it (on it, or it as 3rd / 5th: "D dark over F"). MAIN to hear each, YES keeps. The bar's slot fills; the next bar is next.
  • The key fills in by itself (top bar + sure bars). When two are equally likely it asks one question: "B OR Bb?" (play it, answer) or "DRONE D OR A: WHICH RESTS?".
  • HOLD TEST (no clear bass): a tapped note drones; YES = never sounds wrong → bright or dark? → home or its fifth?

LISTEN: from incoming notes

Play the loop in a few times (a transcribed stem, a bassline, your own playing). It drops glitches and ghost overtones, keeps what repeats, finds the loop length (1/2/4/8 bars), the notes, home from the bass, and a chord per bar. Bass only: PLAIN (bass = root) or TWIST (bass = 3rd / 5th: F/A, Dm/F…). Without the Move's clock it finds the tempo itself (115–128 BPM).

The controls in these modes

  • The four buttons by the OLED, top to bottom: SLOTS (the pads ↔ the 16 slots) · NO · YES · MODES. The OLED names what NO and YES do right now (FITS, KEEP, ADD, TAP…).
  • MAIN: turn = the mode's value (table) · click = play / stop the loop. Middle knob: turn = the setting shown bottom-right · click = the next one · hold = tap "1" (the bar starts here).

Spice (GROW, MAIN turn)

0exactly what you entered (A A, literal)
1–2shape only: thin A / full A', a flip at the end, a held top note, Early (chords a 16th ahead)
3–4+ a way back at phrase ends (bVII, v7, bVI→bVII, V9sus), third swaps, split bars
5–6+ one outside chord per 8 (IV, bIImaj7…), one shape over many basses, a stuck low note
7+ slide shapes (parallel chords), one chromatic-third chord; may make A A B A

Slot 1, the A you entered and locked slots never change. No diminished chords, ≤2 outside chords in a row, outside chords always lead on smoothly.

MELODY

  • Pads: the 5-note scale over 2+ octaves (NOTES = all 7). Nothing can clash.
  • YES (TAP): tap a rhythm on any pad over the next bar; it picks the notes. NO: a whole new line.
  • Voices: lead hook (2–4 notes, repeats) · flute (long, stepwise, grace notes) · house piano (octaves, call & response) · arp (the chord's notes in order).
  • Form A A' A B: on-beat notes are chord notes; it ends on root, 3rd or 5th.

Takes, capture, print

  • Every change is a take (32 kept, starred never age out, saved through power-off). Hold NO = step back one take every 0.6 s.
  • MODES → 16 A/B: the previous take, on the next bar line (again = back) · MODES → 15 CAPTURE: the last bars you played on the pads become the loop.
  • Print to the Move: arm a clip recording, MODES → 14 PRINT: the loop plays once from the next bar line (or from the Move's start), then stops.
  • NEW SONG forgets the key and the votes (the old loop stays in TAKES). The song profile saves itself; there is no save button.
